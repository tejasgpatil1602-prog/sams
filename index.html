Features

Everything a season needs, in one record

Each expense, schedule and harvest is linked to a crop, so the totals for every crop are always current.

01

Farms and crops

Record each plot with its area. Add crops by season and year, with sowing and expected harvest dates.

02

Expense tracking

Log spending in fixed categories such as seed, fertilizer, labour and irrigation. Filter by crop or month.

03

Fertilizer schedule

Plan applications with dates and quantities. The dashboard lists upcoming and overdue tasks until you mark them done or skipped.

04

Harvest and sales

Record quantity harvested, quantity sold, the rate and the buyer. Revenue is calculated automatically.

05

Crop-wise reports

See total cost, revenue, estimated profit, cost per acre and yield per acre for each crop, plus monthly expense totals.

06

Master data control

An administrator keeps the crop and fertilizer lists consistent, so the same crop is never entered under two names.

How it works

From first sowing to final sale

Add your farm

Enter the farm name, village or district, and total area in acres.

Add your crops

Choose the crop, season and area. Each crop gets its own record.

Record the season

Log expenses, plan fertilizer tasks and mark them as they are completed.

Read the result

Record the harvest and sale. The report shows profit or loss per crop.

Crop-wise summary

Numbers that add up, without manual work

SAMS works out the totals on the server, so every screen shows the same result.

  • Total cost is the sum of all expenses for the crop.
  • Revenue is quantity sold multiplied by the sale rate.
  • Estimated profit is revenue minus total cost.
  • Cost and yield per acre divide the totals by the crop area.

Security and privacy

Your farm data belongs to you

Farmers' names, land details and money records are sensitive, so access is restricted from the start.

A

Protected passwords

Passwords are stored only as irreversible hashes, never in plain text.

B

Private records

A farmer sees only their own farms, crops, expenses and harvests. Admins see account details only.

C

Checked input

Every amount, date and quantity is validated before it is saved, and text is shown as plain text.

Important: SAMS is a record keeping and scheduling tool. It does not recommend fertilizer types, doses, timing or pesticides. Enter quantities from your soil test report or an agricultural officer. Profit figures are estimates based only on what you record.
